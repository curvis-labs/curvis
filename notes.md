Curvis 0.1.0b1 (v0.1.0-beta).

Download the installer for your computer:

- **Windows**: `Curvis-0.1.0b1-setup.exe`
- **macOS** (Apple silicon): `Curvis-0.1.0b1-macos-arm64.dmg`
- **Linux**: `curvis_0.1.0b1_amd64.deb` (Debian, Ubuntu) or `Curvis-0.1.0b1-x86_64.AppImage`

The installers are not signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn before the first start.
[Getting started](https://github.com/curvis-labs/curvis/blob/v0.1.0-beta/docs/getting-started.md)
explains how to install, and how to go from a CSV file to an exported graph.
